About EPF Malaysia

The Employees Provident Fund (EPF) is Malaysia’s statutory retirement savings scheme that helps employees build long‑term retirement income. Members and their employers make monthly contributions each month that accumulate and earn dividends.

Effective 11 May 2024, EPF restructured the accounts for members below age 55 into three accounts: Akaun Persaraan (for retirement savings), Akaun Sejahtera (for medium‑term needs), and Akaun Fleksibel (for flexible access to savings as needed). New contributions are credited into these accounts at 75 %, 15 %, and 10 % respectively.

  • Akaun Persaraan: Designed for long‑term retirement accumulation.
  • Akaun Sejahtera: Helps support housing, education, healthcare, and other medium‑term needs.
  • Akaun Fleksibel: A new account that members can access more easily for short‑term needs.

All accounts continue to earn annual dividend credits on balances. The purpose of dividends and contributions is to support retirement readiness for members.
